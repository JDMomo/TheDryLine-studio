# Pass 61+ renderer notes

The corrected process uses a 4x raster proof before scoring. Character construction is explicit rather than oval-based.

Wanderer required authored parts: instrument body, neck/pegs/strings, two scarf tails, three cloak layers, two sleeves, two hands, face, hair, collar, brim, crown, frog body/head/eyes/legs, belt, two legs, two boots. Strider uses articulated legs, layered shell/plate/eye/pincer and rivet details. Gearling uses key, two legs/feet, two arms/fists, layered boiler body, clock face/hands, cap and rivet/seam details.

Stroke rule: dense fine hatching at 4x, shadow-side density first, light side left open, secondary cross-hatch only in darkest hard-material areas. Part boundaries use thin interior lines; the silhouette contour carries the strongest weight.

Every scored pass must have a PNG under `lab/lineart/passes/`; a score without that proof is invalid.
